Privacy Policy

Prepaid User (Android app, package com.graydentech.prepaiduserelectricity)
Effective date: 10 June 2026

Prepaid User helps you track your prepaid electricity meter balance, log purchases, and analyze your usage. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

  • Your meter readings, purchases, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We (the developer) never receive, see, or store your data on any server.
  • Optional backups go to your own Google Drive, protected by your Google account.
  • The app shows ads via Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers as described below.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following information in a private database on your device only: meter balance readings, electricity purchase records (amount, units, vendor fee), and your preferences (currency, cost per unit, theme, notification settings). This data never leaves your device except through the backup features described below, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use the in-app Factory Reset.

Backups to your Google Drive

Two optional mechanisms keep your data safe, both storing it in your own Google account — never with us:

  • Android Auto Backup — Android may automatically back up the app's data to your Google Drive so it can be restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. This is managed by Android and can be turned off in your device's system settings (Settings > Google > Backup).
  • Manual backup (Settings > Backup & Restore) — if you choose to, the app uploads a backup file to a hidden, app-only folder in your Google Drive (the Drive appDataFolder). You authorize this with your Google account, and only this app can read that folder. You can delete the backup at any time from Google Drive settings (Manage apps), or by revoking the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

The developer has no access to your Google account or your backups.

Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and coarse device data to serve and measure ads, as described in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before serving ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Manage privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet / network state — to load ads and to upload/download your Google Drive backup when you request it.
  • Notifications — to show the optional daily reminder to record your meter reading. You can disable this in the app or in system settings.
  • Run at startup — only to re-schedule your daily reminder after the device reboots.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics SDK operated by us, and no servers. We cannot identify you and we do not sell or share any personal information. The only third party processing data in the app is Google (AdMob ads and, if you use it, your own Google Drive for backups).

Children

The app is a utility for managing household electricity and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

  • On the device: use Settings > Reset Options > Factory Reset in the app, or simply uninstall it.
  • Drive backup: remove it via Google Drive settings > Manage apps, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Changes to this policy
